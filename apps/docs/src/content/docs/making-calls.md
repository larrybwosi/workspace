# Making Calls & Screen Sharing

Scrymechat includes built-in high-definition voice calling, video conferencing, and interactive screen sharing powered by low-latency WebRTC streams.

---

## 1. Starting a Voice or Video Call

You can start calls directly from any Direct Message (DM) or workspace channel.

### Step-by-step Instructions

1. Navigate to the desired DM or Channel.
2. Look at the top right header bar of the chat view:
   - Click the **Phone Icon** (`📞`) to start an Audio-only call.
   - Click the **Video Icon** (`📹`) to start a Video call.
3. Your browser will prompt for microphone and camera permissions.
4. Active channel participants will see a **Join Call** banner atop the channel.

> INFO: Browser Permissions: Ensure you grant camera and microphone access when prompted by your web browser!

---

## 2. Screen Sharing & Collaboration

Present documents, slide decks, or live code during a call.

### How to Share Your Screen

1. In the active call bar, click the **Screen Share** icon (`🖥️`).
2. Choose whether to share your **Entire Screen**, a **Specific Window**, or a **Browser Tab**.
3. Click **Share**.
4. Teammates can view your screen stream in real-time with full HD resolution.

---

## 3. Call Controls Quick Reference

| Control | Action | Keyboard Shortcut |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mute / Unmute** | Toggle microphone input | `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + M` |
| **Video On / Off** | Toggle video camera stream | `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + V` |
| **Screen Share** | Start or stop sharing your screen | `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + S` |
| **Leave Call** | Disconnect from active session | `Esc` |

## Troubleshooting Calls

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Microphone Not Found"** | Browser blocked media device access. | Check browser address bar settings and allow microphone permissions. |
| **"Poor Connection Alert"** | High network latency or bandwidth congestion. | Turn off video camera stream to save bandwidth. |
| **"Screen Share Blank"** | System permission missing on OS level. | Enable Screen Recording permissions in system settings (e.g. macOS Privacy Settings). |
